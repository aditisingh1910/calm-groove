Daily Tips

Breathe

Try a simple 4-4-4 exercise: inhale for 4, hold for 4, exhale for 4 — repeat 4 times.

Move

Walk for 10 minutes outside or do gentle stretches to reset your nervous system.

Connect

Reach out to a friend, or write one line in a gratitude journal.

Mindful Breathing

Sit comfortably, close your eyes, and focus on the sensation of breath at the nostrils for 2–5 minutes.

Body Scan

Slowly move attention from toes to head, noticing tension and gently releasing it as you breathe out.

Grounding Trick

Use the 5-4-3-2-1 method: name 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.

Progressive Muscle Relaxation

Tense each muscle group 5 seconds, then release — move from feet up to the face to reduce physical stress.

Guided Imagery

Visualize a calm place in detail — the sights, sounds and textures — for 3–7 minutes to soothe the mind.

Short Practices

When time is tight, do 1–2 minutes of mindful breathing or a single body scan prompt to reset quickly.

Consistency Over Length

Short daily routines (5–10 minutes) beat occasional long sessions; pick a time and make it a micro-habit.
